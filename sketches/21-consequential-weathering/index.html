consequential weathering

When we ask ‘Who has the right to control and change the weather?’, we should ask ‘Who is already interfering in the weather at large scales?’ Because can we really achieve and talk about a weather commons, if all systems it is entangled with are extracted and manipulated across scales?

This sketch will look at extractive open-pit mines, especially pits flooded with river or groundwater, and how they already interfere with the atmosphere, from the ground up through its vertical layers. A weather commons must address this too.
